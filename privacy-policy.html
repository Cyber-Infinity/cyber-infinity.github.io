Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyber Infinity collects, uses, protects, and processes information when you use our websites, applications, services, and supported integrations.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cyber Infinity.

Cyber Infinity is a digital services and technology business providing social media management, account security and recovery services, digital marketing services, and technology solutions, including integrations with platforms such as Meta and WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyber Infinity ("Cyber Infinity," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our websites, applications, services, and integrations, including applications that use the Meta Platform and WhatsApp Business Platform.

By using our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect or receive the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and Business Information

  • Name
  • Business or organization name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business contact information
  • Business account information
  • Information required to configure or manage supported integrations

2.2 Meta and WhatsApp Information

If you authorize Cyber Infinity to access or integrate with Meta products or WhatsApp Business Platform, we may receive information that you authorize Meta to provide to our application.

  • Meta account or business identifiers
  • Facebook Page information
  • Instagram account information where applicable
  • WhatsApp Business Account information
  • WhatsApp business phone number information
  • Messaging-related information necessary to provide the requested service
  • Access tokens or other authentication information required for authorized API functionality

We only request and use permissions that are necessary for the functionality of our application and services.

2.3 Messages and Customer Information

Where our application is configured to provide WhatsApp Business or CRM functionality, we may process information contained in messages, conversations, contacts, or other business communications.

This information is processed on behalf of the business using our service and is used to provide the requested functionality, such as communication management, customer support, CRM functionality, automation, analytics, or other authorized business operations.

2.4 Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Log information
  • Application usage information
  • Security and diagnostic information

3. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate our services
  • To configure and maintain Meta and WhatsApp integrations
  • To provide WhatsApp Business Platform functionality
  • To manage business communications and customer conversations
  • To provide CRM-related functionality
  • To authenticate users and maintain account security
  • To respond to support requests
  • To troubleshoot technical issues
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate unauthorized access or abuse
  • To maintain and improve our services
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Meta Platform Data

When you connect a Meta account, Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business Account, or other supported Meta asset to our application, we process information made available through the Meta Platform according to the permissions you grant.

We use Meta Platform information only to provide the functionality requested by the user or business.

We do not sell Meta Platform data to third parties.

We do not use Meta Platform data for unrelated advertising purposes.

5. WhatsApp Data

If you use our WhatsApp-related services, we may process WhatsApp Business information and messages as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

We do not sell WhatsApp message content or customer information.

Businesses using our services are responsible for ensuring that their use of WhatsApp and customer data complies with applicable laws and Meta/WhatsApp policies.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, protect our services, or operate supporting infrastructure.

We do not sell personal information or Meta Platform data.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:
